# Licensing status

This repository combines source data maintained by DNIT with derived research data produced by the manuscript authors.

## DNIT source workbooks

Files under `data/raw/dnit/` are redistributed with attribution to the Brazilian National Department of Transport Infrastructure (DNIT). No ownership over those files is claimed here, and this repository does not replace the official source or its applicable terms:

- https://servicos.dnit.gov.br/dadospnct
- https://www.gov.br/dnit/pt-br/dadosabertos

## Derived research data

No blanket license has yet been assigned to files under `data/processed/` and `data/simulation-results/`. Public visibility alone does not grant permission for unrestricted reuse. Before the first versioned release, the authors should select an explicit data license, such as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), subject to agreement among the authors and compatibility with the source-data terms.
